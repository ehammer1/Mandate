MandateLive public dashboard

What's happening, in public

Every number here is read directly from the public blockchain, so anyone can check it. No sign-in, and nothing here identifies a voter.

At a glance

Polls

Most requested

Petitions residents are signing, ranked by how close each is to the signatures that open a vote by itself. Signatures are anonymous.

Civic budget rounds

Residents spread equal civic credits across proposals; the most-backed are funded until the budget runs out.

Council: changes to who runs the system

Admin power belongs to a council. Every change is proposed in public, needs several members' approval, then waits before it can run. Any one member can cancel it during the wait.

Mandates

Goals set by public vote or market decision, with a deadline and independent checks that they were met.

The community's voting rules

Changed only by a public rule-change poll with a supermajority, within fixed limits.

Officials and delegates

Every official account, by name and office, how often its holder sided with residents' results, and emergency measures that must be ratified or lapse.

Accountability scorecard

Everyone responsible for a mandate, and how they are doing. Repeated overdue work can be met with a petition to pause, revise or reassign it.

Who can add people to the voter list

Watchdog alerts

An independent watchdog checks every change every 30 minutes and flags anything that moves power or looks unusual.
