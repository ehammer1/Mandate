Privacy policy

Last updated 9 November 2026 · Pilot on a test network

Promissa lets people vote on public decisions without revealing how they voted. It is designed to collect as little about you as possible.

Your vote

Votes are cast with a zero-knowledge proof. The proof shows that an eligible member voted, and that nobody voted twice, without showing which member cast which vote. Ballots are recorded on a public blockchain. Nobody, including the people running Promissa, can link a ballot to you.

Your voter key

Your voter key is created on your device and stays there: in your passkey, in your browser, or in a backup code you keep. Mandate's servers never receive it.

Signing up with Google, Microsoft or email

  • We use the sign-in only to check that you control the account, and that the account has not been used to join before.
  • We receive your email address and whether the provider has verified it. We do not receive your password, contacts, files or anything else.
  • We do not keep your email address. We record only a one-way, keyed fingerprint of it on the blockchain, which cannot be turned back into the address. Its only use is to stop one account joining twice.
  • Email sign-up codes are sent through our email provider (Resend). Codes expire after 10 minutes and are stored only as a hash.
  • Server logs record that a sign-up happened and its transaction, with email addresses masked. Logs are kept by our hosting provider (Render) for a limited time.

What is public

The blockchain record shows that a membership was added, at what level (basic or full rights), by which sign-up method, and when. It does not show your name or email.

What we never do

We do not sell or share personal data, show advertising, or use tracking cookies or analytics.

Your choices

You can delete your voter key from your device at any time. To ask about your data or to be removed from the voter list, email ehammerp1@gmail.com. Anything already written to the blockchain is permanent, which is why we never write personal details there.

Source code

Visit counter

To know roughly how many people use the site, we may count page visits with GoatCounter, a privacy-friendly counter. It uses no cookies and stores no personal information: only totals such as page views, the site you came from, country, screen size and browser. It never sees how you vote or anything you type. It does not run if your browser asks sites not to track you ("Do Not Track" or "Global Privacy Control").
