We the sovereign people, on the record.

Vote privately. Track every promise. Keep it all on the public record.

Promissa Protocol lets a city, school board, union or association ask its members real questions, count every vote once, and follow each decision through to the end, in one open, checkable system.

For members

  • Vote from your phone with Face ID or a fingerprint: no app store, no fees
  • Nobody can see how you voted
  • Change your vote if you were pressured (where the poll allows)
  • Vote on paper or by mail instead, still counted once
  • Start petitions; check your vote was counted

For organizations

  • Yes/No and ranked-choice polls, with your own rules
  • Whole membership, ID-checked members, or one district or ward
  • Paper and online results combined automatically
  • Decisions become mandates with owners, deadlines and progress
  • English and Spanish

Why trust it

  • Results, records and changes are public; anyone can download them
  • Admin changes need several approvals and a public wait
  • An independent watchdog reports every change of power
  • Open-source code anyone can inspect

Run an 8-week pilot

Weeks 1 to 2: set up and train. Weeks 3 to 4: members join; first online poll. Weeks 5 to 6: online and paper vote; track one decision. Weeks 7 to 8: survey members, review, decide what's next. Needs a sponsor, a pilot lead, two clerks and a small council; setup and support are provided.
