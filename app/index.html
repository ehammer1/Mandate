    Council

    Government bodies and boards

    Set up a whole organization

    Pick what kind of organization this is: it suggests the bodies it needs, how an item moves between them, who signs, and voting rules that suit it. Change anything before you create it.

    Or set up one body at a time. Each body sets its own rules; later changes never affect items already on the agenda.

    Seats

    Roles and alternates

    Chair: runs the agenda and can withdraw items before voting. Clerk: publishes the agenda, records votes taken in the room, records closed sessions. Staff: drafts items for members to sponsor. Roles can end on a date. Self-governed bodies can also vote on these themselves.

    Officials

    Register an official's public account: everything it does is then attributed to them by name and office.

    Record where you stand on a poll (for example your council vote on the same question). It is public and compared with residents' results.

    Declare an emergency measure

    Ratify or it lapses: a ratification vote opens at once. Unless residents ratify the measure before the window ends, it is recorded as lapsed.

    Previous council

    Mandate moved to a new council. These last changes on the previous one finish the move; once they have run, this section can be ignored.

    Hand play credits to the council

    This account still owns the play-credits contract. Hand it to the council so no single key can mint credits.

    Give or remove a role

    Changes go to the council as a proposal: other members approve, then there is a public waiting period before they take effect. Removing a role needs approvals but no wait.
