Welcome back

New here?

Your phone first asks to save a passkey: that's your private voter key, unlocked with Face ID or your fingerprint. Then you'll get a 6-digit code by email. You can also join with a Google or Microsoft account, or in person at the city clerk or a library.

Your key syncs through your Apple, Google or Microsoft account, so your phone and computer vote as the same person. It is never sent to us.

Clerk desk

Open the desk once per shift. Your account signs a short message so the ID service knows this is an approved desk. Nothing is stored on this device.

1. Voter code

Ask the person to open "My voter code" in the Mandate app and show you the QR code.

2. Check their ID

  • The photo matches the person in front of you.
  • The ID has not expired.
  • The address on the ID, or a proof of address (utility bill, lease, voter card), is in the city.

Only a scrambled fingerprint of the number is kept, so the same ID can't be used for two voter codes. The number itself is never stored or published.
