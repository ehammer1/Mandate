# Countdown alerts

Every deadline in Mandate has a live countdown: polls, agenda votes, sign-or-veto deadlines,
emergency changes that must be ratified, council changes, mandates and budget rounds.
Countdowns turn amber under a day and red under an hour.

Three ways to be alerted before a deadline:

1. **In the app.** On the home screen, **Needs attention**, **Reminders**: choose 1 week,
   1 day, 8 hours, 1 hour and/or 15 minutes before, and whether to be alerted about urgent
   items, items you follow (tap **🔔 Follow**), or everything. Alerts show on screen and, if
   you allow it, as notifications. They arrive while Mandate is open or installed on the home
   screen (on iPhone: Share, Add to Home Screen). Settings stay on your device.
2. **One item on your calendar.** **📅 Add to calendar** on any item adds it to your phone's
   calendar with the same alerts, so they arrive even when Mandate is closed.
3. **Every deadline on your calendar.** Subscribe to the calendar feed (Dashboard, Stay
   informed). Each deadline has alerts 1 day, 8 hours and 1 hour before. On iPhone, turn off
   **Remove Alerts** for the subscription (Settings, Calendar, Accounts) or iOS drops them.
   Whoever runs the watchdog can change the feed's alert times with `ALARM_HOURS`
   (for example `ALARM_HOURS=168,24,8,1`).
